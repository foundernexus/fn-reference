Most founders asking this are deciding whether a product can replace a seat. It cannot learn who buys, and it cannot close. Use it on research and first drafts, next to a person who already sells. Score it the way you score a human SDR: quota, ramp, and raw pipeline. This is not a vendor ranking.

:::takeaways
- Do not buy an AI SDR to discover the motion. SaaStr: close the first customers yourself, then hire two closers. Those first reps should have been account executives, not only SDRs.
- Bridge Group, 2025 report (351 B2B companies): **60%** of SDRs at quota, **$80K** median OTE, **3.0** month average ramp, **$3.78M** raw pipeline per SDR, **40%** median attrition in 2024. An AI SDR was its own category for **1%** of respondents, the first year they tracked it.
- That sample is not a seed company. Median revenue **$47M**, median ASP **$50K**, 83% B2B SaaS. Do not paste $3.78M onto a Series A plan.
- Gartner, 25 June 2025: over **40%** of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, from cost, unclear value, or weak risk controls. A forecast about projects. Not a measured AI SDR failure rate. Do not merge it with the 1%.
- Put AI on research and drafts. Keep a person on inbound and on the close ([FounderNexus session](https://www.foundernexus.com?utm_source=founderdecisions&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=library)).
:::

## What you are actually comparing

:::highlight
An AI SDR is a prep tool on a motion you already understand. It is not the person who learns the buyer, and it is not the closer.
:::

| Job | AI | Person | Who says so |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Learn who buys, why, and at what price | No | Founder, until the motion transfers | SaaStr: close the first ~10 yourself before any sales hire |
| First sales hire | No. Not the first seat | Two closers you would buy from, with AE experience | SaaStr: at least 18 months as an AE, not just an SDR. Hire two, not one |
| Research, lists, first-draft sequences | Yes, if a person reviews before it sends | The seller who already knows the buyer | [FounderNexus session](https://www.foundernexus.com?utm_source=founderdecisions&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=library) |
| Chatbot in front of inbound | No | A person answers. Speed is the job | FounderNexus session |
| Closing a deal large enough to matter | No | Founder or AE | SaaStr; FounderNexus session |
| Replacing an SDR headcount line | Only after you can score it on the table below | The seat still has a quota, a ramp, and attrition | Bridge Group |

This page does not cite vendor tests of AI SDR reply rates or cost per meeting. The ones in circulation do not publish a sample, a method, and a date you can check. Use the Bridge Group scoreboard instead.

## When the AI SDR is the wrong buy

:::highlight
Buy it to take prep off a seller who already knows the motion. Do not buy it to skip that seller.
:::

1. **You have not closed the first customers.** SaaStr: close the first 10 or so yourself. You cannot teach a motion you have not run, and a tool cannot learn it for you.
2. **The first seat you would buy is a meeting-booker.** SaaStr's bar for the first reps is 18 months as an AE, not an SDR-only resume, and two of them so you can tell whether the motion transferred.
3. **Inbound is the leak.** Do not put a chatbot in front of it. A person still answers. A FounderNexus session put the same line on the close: deals large enough to matter stay with a human, and the useful frame is one seller made more effective, not a team deleted.
4. **The dashboard you will manage is meetings booked.** Bridge Group scores the human seat on quota (60% at it), ramp (3.0 months), Stage 0 meetings held (10 a month), and raw pipeline ($3.78M). If the pilot cannot be read on that scoreboard, you are not comparing SDRs.
5. **The plan has no kill criteria.** Gartner, 25 June 2025, forecasts that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027 because of escalating costs, unclear business value, or inadequate risk controls. That is a forecast about agentic projects in general. It is not an AI SDR win rate, and it is not the Bridge Group 1%.

## Worked situations

:::highlight
Labeled sketches. Not a company. The thresholds are the sources above, not a new survey.
:::

**You have closed fewer than 10 customers, there is no sales rep, and the board wants an AI SDR that books meetings overnight.** SaaStr says you are still in the founder-close phase. There is no human seat to replace, and nothing for the tool to learn from. Bridge Group's $80K OTE and $3.78M pipeline describe companies at a $47M median revenue and a $50K median ASP. Keep selling until you can teach the motion.

**Two closers are working, inbound sits for days, and the proposal is a chatbot plus an AI SDR instead of another person.** Put a person on inbound. If you still pilot AI, limit it to research and first drafts for those closers, with a human review before anything sends. Count Stage 0 meetings the AE will actually take, and raw pipeline, the way Bridge Group scores an SDR. A meeting the AE will not take does not count. Headcount comes later, and the title question is on the [Series A leadership sequence](/library/hiring/series-a-leadership-hiring-sequence/) if the board is already talking executives.
