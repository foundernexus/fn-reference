Founder-CEOs at venture-backed US startups average **$153,000** in cash salary at seed, **$203,000** at Series A and **$216,000** at Series B, on Kruze Consulting's 2026 payroll data. A non-founder C-level hire gets about **1%** of fully diluted equity. That is Carta's median initial grant and the typical grant in Index Ventures' guide. VPs get much less: Carta's median VP grant is **0.17%**, and Index puts a Series A VP at **0.3–0.8%**. Past the CEO, CTO, COO and VP Sales, the public cash data thins out fast. There is no reliable public benchmark yet for VP Engineering, VP Product, VP Marketing or VP People cash by stage. Not legal, tax, or compensation advice.

:::takeaways
- Kruze payroll, 2026: CEOs average **$153,000 / $203,000 / $216,000** at Seed / A / B. CTOs lead at seed (**$155,000**). COOs lead at Series A (**$227,000**) and Series B (**$246,000**).
- Carta, VC-backed medians across all stages: non-founder CEO about **4.8%** at hire, other C-level **1%**, SVP **0.36%**, VP **0.17%**.
- Index rule of thumb: VP **0.3–0.8%** at Series A and **0.2–0.7%** at Series B. C-level **0.8–1.5%**.
- Executive grants are shrinking. Carta's median VP grant is down **9.7%** since Q1 2024 and the C-suite median is down **2.8%**.
- Budget **25–35%** above base for taxes and benefits (Kruze). Three Series A seats at **$599,000** of base cost about **$778,700** a year at a 30% load.
:::

## Cash salary by role and stage

:::highlight
Kruze's payroll data covers the CEO, CTO and COO. Search-firm medians cover VP Sales. For the other seats, one recruiter's ranges are all that is public.
:::

| Role | Seed | Series A | Series B |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CEO, average¹ | **$153,000** | **$203,000** | **$216,000** |
| CEO, typical range¹ | $130,000–$170,000 | $180,000–$230,000 | $200,000–$260,000 |
| CTO, average² | **$155,000** | **$196,000** | **$238,000** |
| COO, average² | **$144,000** | **$227,000** | **$246,000** |
| CFO or finance leader³ | $150,000–$175,000 | No reliable public benchmark yet | $250,000–$350,000+ (B and later) |
| VP Sales, base / median OTE⁴ | $140K / $180K (first sales hire) | $160K / $260K (head of sales){br}$200K / $325K (first VP, A to B) | $220K / $385K |
| VP Engineering, Product, Marketing, People⁵ | No reliable public benchmark yet | No reliable public benchmark yet | No reliable public benchmark yet |

- ¹ Kruze Consulting, 2026 Startup CEO Salary Report (April 2026; founder salary page updated November 2026). Anonymized payroll from 450+ funded US startups. Cash salary only, no equity. Overall average **$165,000** (up from **$161,000** in 2025), median **$159,000**. Series B is still below its 2022 peak of **$262,000**.
- ² Kruze Consulting, Startup C-Suite Salary Guide 2026 (10 August 2026). Same payroll data. Overall medians: CTO **$166,000**, COO **$150,000**. Kruze's founder salary page describes these as technical and operating founders.
- ³ Kruze Consulting, in-house finance team guide (2026; built on Robert Half, Glassdoor, Salary.com and ZipRecruiter 2026 data plus Kruze payroll). Seed-stage finance leaders often earn $150,000–$175,000. Series B+ CFOs earn $250,000–$350,000+ in cash, often with **0.5–2%** equity. Full-time CFO or VP Finance base across stages: $175,000–$350,000+. No Series A figure.
- ⁴ UltraTalent, VP Sales Compensation Benchmarks 2026 (updated 13 November 2026). An executive search firm consolidating Pavilion 2026 (412 companies), Carta Q1 2026 and The CRO Report (704 job postings). OTE figures are medians. Pay mix runs 50/50 at seed and Series A, 60/40 at Series B.
- ⁵ No payroll dataset or open survey publishes these seats by stage. The only published ranges come from one executive search firm (next table).

### Search-firm ranges for the other seats

Majhi Group published these base salary ranges in June 2026. They come from 25+ of its own VP and C-suite placements, cross-referenced with Korn Ferry, Radford/Aon and LinkedIn Salary Insights. Treat them as one recruiter's ranges. They are not a market median.

| Role | Seed / pre-A | Series A | Series B |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VP Engineering | $170K–$200K | $190K–$260K | $230K–$310K |
| VP Product / CPO | $160K–$195K | $180K–$240K | $215K–$280K |
| CMO / VP Marketing | $145K–$175K | $170K–$225K | $200K–$265K |
| Chief People Officer / VP People | $145K–$180K | $175K–$230K | $210K–$275K |
| CFO | $160K–$200K | $200K–$270K | $240K–$320K |

Majhi puts annual bonuses at **15–25%** of base for VPs outside sales and **20–30%** for the CFO and COO.

### Carta medians at hire

Carta's 2026 PE Executive Equity Report gives the median initial grant at VC-backed startups on Carta, using 2022–2025 data. It does not split the medians by stage.

| Role | Median initial grant (% of fully diluted equity) |
| --- | --- |
| Non-founder CEO | about **4.8%** |
| Non-founder C-level | **1%** |
| SVP | **0.36%** |
| VP | **0.17%** |

Read the **0.17%** VP median as a midpoint across seed through late stage. Index's **0.3–0.8%** is a Series A band. A Series A VP offer under 0.3% sits below Index's range, even if it clears Carta's median.

Founders hold far more than any hire. In Pave's data (April 2024), the median founder CEO owns about **30%** at seed. Carta's Founder Ownership Report 2026 puts founding teams at about **56%** after seed and about **36%** after Series A.

## Executive pay is moving down

:::highlight
Founder cash is rising. Grants to hired executives are shrinking.
:::

Carta's State of Startup Compensation for H1 2026 shows the split. The median initial grant to a VP is **9.7%** smaller than in Q1 2024. Over the same period, upper mid-level individual contributors saw their median grant grow **25.4%**. Carta also reports that salary benchmarks for executives declined in 2026. In a November 2026 follow-up, Carta put the drop in the median C-suite grant since the start of 2024 at **2.8%**, with VPs and SVPs down by more.

Founder cash went the other way in Kruze's data. The average CEO salary rose from **$161,000** in 2025 to **$165,000** in 2026, and Series B pay is still below the **$262,000** peak of 2022.

If a candidate anchors on a 2021 or 2022 offer, show them the date on your source.

## What three Series A seats cost

Editorial arithmetic on the cited figures. Not a company.

A Series A company pays its CEO the Kruze average, hires a CTO at the Kruze average, and hires a first VP Sales at UltraTalent's first-VP median. Kruze says to budget **25–35%** above base for payroll taxes, insurance and benefits. This example uses 30%.

| Seat | Base | Cost at a 30% load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CEO | $203,000 | $263,900 |
| CTO | $196,000 | $254,800 |
| First VP Sales (base only) | $200,000 | $260,000 |
| Total | **$599,000** | **$778,700** |

Add the VP Sales variable at target (**$125,000**, the gap between the **$325,000** OTE and the **$200,000** base). The three seats then cost about **$903,700** a year at plan, before payroll tax on the commission. That is about **$75,300** a month of burn. Check it against your plan in the [runway calculator](/tools/runway-calculator/).

## How to set the offer

:::steps Set the offer
1. Name the stage you are hiring for. Use the last round you closed.
2. Set cash from the dataset that covers the seat: Kruze for the CEO, CTO and COO, UltraTalent for VP Sales, Kruze's finance guide for the CFO. Label anything else as one search firm's range.
3. Set the grant from Index's band for your stage. Then note where it sits against Carta's all-stage median.
4. Check the grant against your remaining pool in the [executive equity calculator](/tools/executive-equity-calculator/). [Size next year's pool from your hiring plan](/library/equity/option-pool-from-hiring-plan/) before you make the offer.
5. Ask your lead investor for a cut of Pave's VCECS. Pave distributes it to its VC partners and participating companies only. The 2025 edition is a November 2025 snapshot drawn from 57,000+ executive records at 8,000+ organizations.
6. Write the phase into the offer. Say which chapter the role is for.
7. Take cash and grant to the board with the source and date next to each number.
:::

For the order of hires, read [Series A leadership hiring sequence](/library/hiring/series-a-leadership-hiring-sequence/). For the first sales leader, read [when to hire the first VP of Sales](/library/hiring/when-to-hire-first-vp-sales/). If the CFO seat is the question, compare [fractional vs full-time CFO](/library/finance/fractional-vs-full-time-cfo/) first.

## Where the public data runs out

There is no reliable public benchmark yet for:

- VP Engineering, VP Product, VP Marketing or VP People cash by stage, outside one search firm's ranges.
- CFO cash at Series A.
- Any C-level or VP grant at seed.
- Carta's executive grant medians split by stage.

A [VEN](https://www.venexecs.com/?utm_source=founderdecisions&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=library&utm_content=executive-compensation-benchmarks) session drew one line on executive offers: hire for the phase and say it in the offer. The person who builds a function for this chapter may not be the one who scales it after the next round.
