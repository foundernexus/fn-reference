Fundraising here is three decisions in sequence. First at Seed: which instrument fits the raise (SAFE, priced equity, or note). Then at Series A: whether the machine is underwritable now, with enough cash to negotiate. Then: how you stage the evidence so investors rebuild your cohorts from one source of truth. Seed bet on a team and a thesis. Series A underwrites repeatable, capital-efficient growth (Burkland, 2026). Calendar anniversaries do not. Open the spoke for the question on your desk. Not legal, tax, or investment advice.

:::takeaways
- Seed instrument: pick a post-money SAFE, priced equity, or convertible note by raise size and lead terms. Carta mix and YC mechanics live on [SAFE vs priced round](/library/fundraising/safe-vs-priced-round/).
- Timing: raise when you can show a repeatable engine, not because the seed anniversary hit. Burkland: many SaaS conversations still cluster around roughly **$1M–$3M** ARR; **NRR** above **100%** is a strong signal; burn multiple under ~**2x** reads efficient for early SaaS. See [when to raise Series A](/library/fundraising/when-to-raise-series-a/).
- Cash posture: under **6** months of runway into the process is a desperation signal; **9–12+** months is stronger (Burkland). Plan **3–6** months from first meetings to close, and start prep **6+** months before outreach.
- Market medians are planning context, not your go/no-go. Carta: median seed → primary A was **616 days** (~20 months) in Q2 2025; Peter Walker reports **1.9 years** for Q4 2025. Bessemer SotC 2023: raise when capital is available and you do not need the money.
- Room: assemble the legal checklist before you sign a term sheet (YC Kwon/Harris: can cut **as much as a week**). Underscore stages access: short deck, then curated partner-pitch room, then full legal only after the term sheet. See [Series A diligence checklist](/library/fundraising/series-a-diligence-checklist/).
- Hygiene: one glossary for ARR, GRR, NRR, CAC, burn, and runway across deck, model, and board pack. Inconsistent definitions are a deal risk on their own (Burkland).
:::

## Stage sketch for the raise

:::highlight
Early: prove the machine and extend cash. Close to outreach: lock the glossary and stage the room. After the term sheet: open confirmatory legal fast.
:::

**Structuring Seed paper.** Prefer a post-money SAFE unless a lead will price preferred stock now ([SAFE vs priced round](/library/fundraising/safe-vs-priced-round/)). Instrument mix and conversion traps live on that spoke. Keep early money on one instrument.

**Still proving the engine (Seed into early A readiness).** Lead with growth cadence, GRR beside NRR, and burn multiple trend (Burkland; Sacks/Craft bands on the [burn multiple](/library/finance/burn-multiple-vs-rule-of-40/) spoke). If two or more core lenses are still bets, keep executing ([when to raise Series A](/library/fundraising/when-to-raise-series-a/)). Carta’s stretching seed-to-A medians are market context, not a reason to force a process.

**Prep window (about 6+ months before outreach).** Lock one metric glossary. Curate the partner-pitch room and finish the YC legal spine with counsel ([diligence checklist](/library/fundraising/series-a-diligence-checklist/)). Clear pending option grants before a term sheet can force a material-event 409A (YC). Target **9–12+** months of runway at first serious partner meetings (Burkland).

**Live process.** Underscore: short deck on first meet; long-form deck plus controlled deal room at partner pitch; full legal only after the term sheet. Share the forecast you run the company on. Bessemer’s fundability frame still holds: raise when you do not need the money (SotC 2023 bands on the readiness spoke).

**Post–term sheet.** Open confirmatory folders organized to YC’s categories so counsel is not inventing a map under deadline. Burkland’s common dilution framing (~**15–25%** including negotiated option-pool expansion) belongs in the model before you negotiate, not as a surprise mid-close.
