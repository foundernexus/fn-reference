Fundraising is a run of separate decisions, from the first angel check to the Series A data room. Seed bets on a team and a thesis. Series A underwrites repeatable, capital-efficient growth (Burkland, 2026). Calendar anniversaries do not. Find the question you are facing below. Not legal, tax, or investment advice.

:::group First money: angels and seed
- [First angel check](/library/fundraising/first-angel-check/): Pre-seed or early seed. Warm intros, active writers, an ask in runway months, a post-money SAFE.
- [How seed VCs decide](/library/fundraising/how-seed-vcs-decide/): Picking funds to pitch. Team first, then product, market, and fit with the fund; valuation last.
- [Seed pitch deck](/library/fundraising/seed-pitch-deck/): Deck draft before the first send. One takeaway per slide, numbers that match the model.
- [Seed fundraising process](/library/fundraising/seed-fundraising-process/): Deck ready. Size the ask to a milestone, filter funds, then meet them in parallel.
:::

:::group Choose the paper
- [SAFE vs priced round](/library/fundraising/safe-vs-priced-round/): Structuring seed paper. Post-money SAFE, priced equity, or a note, by raise size and lead terms.
- [SAFE dilution calculator](/tools/safe-dilution-calculator/): SAFEs signed, a round coming. See each holder's percentage after conversion, the pool, and the new money.
- [Term sheet red flags](/library/fundraising/term-sheet-red-flags/): A lead sent paper. Read each line against the clean set and fight about three issues.
- [Venture debt](/library/fundraising/venture-debt/): An equity round closed recently. Whether a loan fits, how much, and which terms to fight.
:::

:::group Series A
- [When to raise Series A](/library/fundraising/when-to-raise-series-a/): The board asks if you are ready. Score growth, NRR, burn multiple, and runway before you open.
- [Series A diligence checklist](/library/fundraising/series-a-diligence-checklist/): Partner pitches are starting. Build the room before outreach and stage what you share.
:::

:::group Short on cash
- [When runway runs out](/library/fundraising/when-runway-runs-out/): Default dead or payroll at risk. Cut, bridge, extend with revenue, or wind down in order. Pair with the [runway calculator](/tools/runway-calculator/).
:::

:::takeaways Numbers to know
- Timing: raise when you can show a repeatable engine, not because the seed anniversary hit. Burkland: many SaaS conversations still cluster around roughly **$1M–$3M** ARR; **NRR** above **100%** is a strong signal; burn multiple under ~**2x** reads efficient for early SaaS.
- Cash posture: under **6** months of runway into the process is a desperation signal; **9–12+** months is stronger (Burkland). Plan **3–6** months from first meetings to close, and start prep **6+** months before outreach.
- Market medians are planning context, not your go/no-go. Carta: median seed → primary A was **616 days** (~20 months) in Q2 2025; Peter Walker reports **1.9 years** for Q4 2025. Bessemer SotC 2023: raise when capital is available and you do not need the money.
- Room: assemble the legal checklist before you sign a term sheet (YC Kwon/Harris: can cut **as much as a week**).
- Hygiene: one glossary for ARR, GRR, NRR, CAC, burn, and runway across deck, model, and board pack. Inconsistent definitions are a deal risk on their own (Burkland).
:::

A [VEN](https://www.venexecs.com/?utm_source=founderdecisions&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=library) session pushed the same pattern: close early angels with a clean filter and one instrument, pitch seed funds whose lens fits your proof, run outreach in parallel from a filtered list, triage short runway before a panic raise, and open with cash to negotiate. That is session judgment, not a survey.

## What changes by stage

**Closing first angel checks.** Warm intros and active writers before spray lists ([first angel check](/library/fundraising/first-angel-check/)). Default to a post-money SAFE; treat platforms as auxiliary once you have real leads.

**Pitching seed funds.** Qualify each fund’s thesis, check size, and deployment before the pitch, lead with the team, and run each first meeting to earn the second ([how seed VCs decide](/library/fundraising/how-seed-vcs-decide/)).

**Building the seed deck.** One takeaway per slide, revenue-based market size, and an ask tied to a milestone; send a short teaser and keep the appendix for the meeting ([seed pitch deck](/library/fundraising/seed-pitch-deck/)).

**Running the seed raise.** Write the milestone first, filter funds before asking for intros, warm up on a few real meetings, then run the main list in parallel and close each yes the same week ([seed fundraising process](/library/fundraising/seed-fundraising-process/)).

**Structuring Seed paper.** Prefer a post-money SAFE unless a lead will price preferred stock now ([SAFE vs priced round](/library/fundraising/safe-vs-priced-round/)). Instrument mix and conversion traps live on that page. Keep early money on one instrument.

**Right after an equity close.** Decide whether venture debt belongs on top while your position is strongest ([venture debt](/library/fundraising/venture-debt/)). Size from the round and debt service, and negotiate the draw window and default clauses before you sign the term sheet.

**Short runway.** Measure snapshot and default alive/dead, then cut, bridge, extend with revenue, or land soft ([when runway runs out](/library/fundraising/when-runway-runs-out/)). Model cash in the [runway calculator](/tools/runway-calculator/). Do not open a full A process on thin cash; Burkland’s desperation signal lives on that page.

**Still proving the engine (Seed into early A readiness).** Lead with growth cadence, GRR beside NRR, and burn multiple trend (Burkland; Sacks/Craft bands on the [burn multiple](/library/finance/burn-multiple-vs-rule-of-40/) page). If two or more core lenses are still bets, keep executing ([when to raise Series A](/library/fundraising/when-to-raise-series-a/)). Carta’s stretching seed-to-A medians are market context, not a reason to force a process.

**Prep window (about 6+ months before outreach).** Lock one metric glossary. Curate the partner-pitch room and finish the YC legal spine with counsel ([diligence checklist](/library/fundraising/series-a-diligence-checklist/)). Clear pending option grants before a term sheet can force a material-event 409A (YC). Target **9–12+** months of runway at first serious partner meetings (Burkland).

**Live process.** Underscore: short deck on first meet; long-form deck plus controlled deal room at partner pitch; full legal only after the term sheet. Share the forecast you run the company on. Bessemer’s fundability frame still holds: raise when you do not need the money (SotC 2023 bands on the Series A timing page).

**Term sheet in hand.** Confirm pre- vs post-money in writing, model the waterfall and pool, and read the board and vetoes before price ([term sheet red flags](/library/fundraising/term-sheet-red-flags/)). Keep exclusivity short.

**Post–term sheet.** Open confirmatory folders organized to YC’s categories so counsel is not inventing a map under deadline. Burkland’s common dilution framing (~**15–25%** including negotiated option-pool expansion) belongs in the model before you negotiate, not as a surprise mid-close.
