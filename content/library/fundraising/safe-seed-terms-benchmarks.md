The cap on a SAFE tracks the size of the round. In Q3 2026 the median post-money SAFE cap on Carta was **$8.0 million** for rounds under $250,000, **$20.0 million** for $1 million to $2.4 million, and **$40.0 million** for $2.5 million or more. Most SAFEs carry a cap and no discount: **75%** of post-money SAFEs issued in the first three quarters of 2026 were cap only. If a SAFE has both a cap and a discount, the discount is **20%** about two times in three. A priced seed on Carta in Q2 2026 had a median post-money valuation of **$23.9 million**, a **$4.5 million** round and **19.4%** dilution. Not legal or tax advice.

:::takeaways
- Cap by round size, Carta Q3 2026 medians: **$8.0M** under $250K, **$11.5M** at $250K–$499K, **$15.0M** at $500K–$999K, **$20.0M** at $1M–$2.4M, **$40.0M** at $2.5M+.
- Spread is wide. At $2.5M+, Carta's 25th to 75th percentile runs **$20M–$50M** and the 90th percentile is **$100M** (Q1 2025–Q3 2026).
- **92%** of SAFEs issued in Q3 2026 were post-money (Carta). In Wilson Sonsini's deals, **89%** of capped SAFEs were post-money in H1 2026.
- Most SAFEs convert at the cap. Only **29%** of 2026 seed rounds on Carta came in below the company's highest SAFE cap.
- Priced rounds stay clean. Cooley's Q2 2026 deals: **95.8%** with a 1x preference and **96.4%** nonparticipating.
:::

## SAFE valuation caps by round size

:::highlight
Compare your cap with rounds of the same size. A single market median hides a five-fold spread between small and large rounds.
:::

| Round size | Median cap, Q3 2026¹ | 25th–75th percentile² | 90th percentile² |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Under $250K | **$8.0M** | $5.0M–$12.0M | $25.0M |
| $250K–$499K | **$11.5M** | $6.0M–$16.0M | $25.0M |
| $500K–$999K | **$15.0M** | $7.0M–$20.0M | $30.0M |
| $1M–$2.4M | **$20.0M** | $10.0M–$25.0M | $40.0M |
| $2.5M and up | **$40.0M** | $20.0M–$50.0M | $100.0M |

- ¹ Carta, State of Pre-Seed: Q3 2026 (12 November 2026; data as of 9 November 2026). Post-money SAFEs at US startups on Carta, before a priced round. Carta groups every instrument a company raised at the same cap into one round. The median cap rose year over year in every size band. At $2.5M and up it went from **$30 million** in Q3 2025 to **$40 million**, up **33%**.
- ² Same report, percentiles across Q1 2025 to Q3 2026 combined. The median over that longer window is lower than the Q3 2026 median in four of the five bands: $8.0M, $10.0M, $10.0M, $15.0M and $30.0M by band.

Wilson Sonsini reports one median across all SAFE sizes in deals the firm worked on: **$15.0 million** in H1 2026, down from **$20.0 million** in 2025. Its median SAFE raise fell to **$240,000** in Q2 2026, from **$610,000** in Q1, so its sample now leans toward the smallest checks.

Carta's August 2026 cut showed the same climb a quarter earlier. The median cap on SAFEs of $2.5 million or more was **$35 million** in Q2 2026, up **40%** year over year.

### What the cap means for ownership

Editorial arithmetic. Not a company.

On a post-money SAFE, the investor's minimum stake is the check divided by the cap. A **$1.5 million** SAFE round at the **$20.0 million** band median sells at least **7.5%** of the company before the priced round. The same round at the band's 25th percentile cap of **$10.0 million** sells **15%**.

The minimum is the usual outcome. Carta compared each company's highest post-money SAFE cap with its later seed price. In the first three quarters of 2026, **71%** of those seed rounds converted the SAFEs at the cap and **29%** converted below it.

## Discounts, post-money, and MFN terms

:::highlight
Two datasets agree on the shape. Caps dominate, the discount is optional, and 20% is the default rate.
:::

| Term | Carta | Wilson Sonsini |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post-money SAFEs | **92%** of SAFEs issued in Q3 2026 (88% a year earlier)³ | **89%** of capped SAFEs in H1 2026 (81% in 2025)⁴ |
| Cap only | **75%** of post-money SAFEs, Q1–Q3 2026³ | **64%** of SAFEs, H1 2026⁴ |
| Cap and discount | **19%**³ | **21%**⁴ |
| Discount only | **4%**³ | **15%**⁴ |
| Discount set at exactly 20% | **67.7%** of cap-and-discount SAFEs; **55.6%** of discount-only SAFEs⁵ | **68%** of SAFEs with a discount; median rate **20%**⁴ |
| Uncapped MFN SAFE | No reliable public benchmark yet | No reliable public benchmark yet |
| Pro rata side letter | No reliable public benchmark yet | No reliable public benchmark yet |

- ³ Carta, State of Pre-Seed: Q3 2026. Pre-priced rounds only. Cap-and-discount SAFEs were **40%** of post-money SAFEs in 2021 and **24%** in 2025.
- ⁴ Wilson Sonsini, The Entrepreneurs Report: Q2 2026. **85%** of SAFEs had a cap in H1 2026, down from **93%** in 2025. About **32%** of SAFEs with a discount used a rate other than 20%.
- ⁵ Carta, State of Pre-Seed: Q3 2026. Discount rates across Q1 2025 to Q3 2026.

The two firms count different deals. Carta sees companies that run their cap table on its software. Wilson Sonsini sees deals its own lawyers worked. Treat the gap in discount-only SAFEs (**4%** vs **15%**) as a difference between the two samples.

Y Combinator's standard deal is the one public anchor for the uncapped MFN SAFE. YC invests **$500,000**: **$125,000** on a post-money SAFE for **7%**, and **$375,000** on an uncapped SAFE with a most favored nation clause. The MFN SAFE converts on the terms of the lowest-cap SAFE the company issues after the batch starts. YC's example: at a **$15 million** cap the **$375,000** converts to **2.5%**.

## How to use these numbers

:::steps Benchmark your terms
1. Find your band. Use the total you plan to raise at this cap, not the size of one check.
2. Read the median and the 25th to 75th percentile for that band. Half of comparable rounds set a cap inside that range.
3. Divide the round by the cap. That is the minimum you sell, and on Carta's data it is what you sell about seven times in ten.
4. Add the seed round's dilution. Carta's median priced seed takes **19.4%** more.
5. Decide on the discount. A cap alone is the most common structure. If an investor asks for both, 20% is the standard rate.
6. Model the stack in the [SAFE dilution calculator](/tools/safe-dilution-calculator/) before you sign the next SAFE.
7. Take the term sheet to [term sheet red flags](/library/fundraising/term-sheet-red-flags/) when the priced round arrives.
:::

## Where the public data runs out

There is no reliable public benchmark yet for:

- The share of SAFEs that come with a pro rata side letter or an uncapped MFN clause.
- Board seats, protective provisions, or preference terms in seed rounds alone.
- SAFE caps outside the US.
