YPO vs EO vs Hampton vs a venture-scale room

Side-by-side filters for invite-only founder and CEO rooms. Stage fit and peer density first. Empty cells where a source did not publish the number.

Pick the room by who else is in it and how they match your stage. Revenue thresholds, age caps, and chapter geography are filters. They are not the same as a room built for venture-scale founders making fundraising, GTM, and board calls. Sources below are the organizations' own pages unless noted. Empty cells mean that page did not publish the figure.

In short

  • YPO filters for under-45 CEOs at large operating scale ($16M+ sales/service/manufacturing revenue on YPO.org).
  • EO filters for owners/founders at $1M+ revenue, with a separate path for venture-backed companies.
  • Hampton Core is in-person, tech/digital founders, $3M+ revenue or $3M+ raised or a prior >$10M exit, chapter city required.
  • Vistage and Powderkeg are peer-advisory formats for established operators and growth-stage tech leaders. Neither is venture-scale exclusive.
  • A venture-scale room (FounderNexus) filters for venture ambition and stage-matched decisions, not a revenue floor alone.

What to compare

Who gets in. Revenue, headcount, age, ownership, and industry screens change the peer set. A Seed founder raising Series A and a $20M industrial CEO do not face the same board pack.

How the room is built. Forum / Core / Chair groups differ from stage-matched sessions. Mirror-Mentor-Mentee mixes stages on purpose. Stage-pure rooms do the opposite.

What sits between meetings. Some orgs assign a Chair or partner. Some leave you with Slack and chapter events. Some publish neither.

Geography. In-person-only Core or chapter forums lock you to a city. Online sessions remove that penalty and change the relationship density.

If your company is under YPO's revenue bar, YPO is the wrong filter to force. If you need venture peers on a live raise, a broad CEO forum is a different product.

How the filters bite at Seed through Series B

You’re pre-Seed or Seed, venture-backed, under $3M revenue. YPO’s published thresholds are usually out of reach. EO’s venture-backed path can apply if raise and headcount clear. Hampton’s FAQ needs $3M revenue, $3M raised, or a prior >$10M exit, plus a chapter city. Vistage’s $5M revenue bar is usually later. Powderkeg’s published band starts at $1M revenue. FounderNexus pre-approval is experience and venture-scale intent, not a $16M revenue floor.

You’re Series A, ~$2M–$10M ARR, raising or hiring the first VP layer. Hampton Core can fit if you clear $3M revenue or raise and live in-chapter. Powderkeg’s $1M–$100M+ tech band overlaps. EO and Vistage still mix owner-operated and mid-market CEOs unless your chapter is unusually startup-dense. FounderNexus Stage 2 is published as that ARR band.

You’re past $16M revenue, under 45, full P&L CEO. YPO’s published criteria can apply. That peer set is large-company CEOs across industries, not a Seed GTM war room. Use it for CEO craft and network. Do not expect every forum mate to have raised a priced round last year.

What these rooms are not

They are not interchangeable with a board, counsel, or an accelerator. They are not fundraising desks. Warm intros happen inside some of them; none of the public pages above guarantee a raise.

Dues are chapter- and product-specific. YPO and EO publish global dues ranges on third-party or chapter pages that move. Hampton’s FAQ does not list a public dollar figure. This page leaves price cells empty rather than guess.

Worked situations

You’re at $800k ARR, raised a Seed, and your board wants “a peer group.” Check EO’s venture-backed path and FounderNexus pre-approval. Hampton and Vistage’s published revenue bars are above you unless you clear Hampton’s raise or exit alternate. YPO is not the next step.

You’re at $4M ARR in SF, tech, and want monthly in-person accountability. Hampton Core is built for that format. Powderkeg overlaps on stage. Ask each how they mix bootstrapped vs venture-backed in your city group.

You’re at $18M revenue, 60 employees, age 41, industrial SaaS. YPO’s published filter can fit. Decide whether you want a global CEO forum or a venture-stage room for the next raise. Those are different jobs.
