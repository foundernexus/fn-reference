Stage 1 (pre-seed and seed)

Test with money before writing real code

A founder at S1 with an idea must decide whether to build now or validate first because most projects fail from building before thinking.

Options

  • Build immediately from the idea
  • Test a numeric hypothesis with cheap demo artifacts
  • Validate with surveys and interviews only

What mattered

  • Willingness to pay is the signal that counts
  • LTV to CAC must clear four to one
  • Failed predecessors reveal unsolved fatal flaws

What was done

Founders in the sessions tested whether a share of a market would pay a set price, proceeded only above a seventy percent hit rate, and called founders of failed predecessors.

Validate with a numeric hypothesis and real payment before building; a demo people like is not a business.
