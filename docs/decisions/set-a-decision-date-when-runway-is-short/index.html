Set a decision date when runway is short

A founder at S1 or S2 with shrinking runway must decide whether to keep going, sell, or wind down because delay quietly makes the choice for them.

Options

  • Keep waiting and hope a raise lands
  • Set a firm decision date at burn minus five months
  • Wait until cash is nearly gone

What mattered

  • An orderly wind-down takes two to three months of cash
  • The team and pipeline are the only asset a buyer wants
  • Leverage disappears as desperation becomes visible

What was done

Founders in the sessions locked in a decision date five months before cash-out and diagnosed the failure mode with data before choosing a path.

Waiting is itself a decision; set the decision date at five months of runway, not at hope of a raise.
